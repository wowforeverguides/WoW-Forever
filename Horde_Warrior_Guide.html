3. What changed for Warriors

Blizzard's 2 Nov development notes (posted 3 Nov)

  • Defensive Stance threat bonus 30% → 80%; Defiance +7/13/20% threat (was 5/10/15%) Blizzard. Blizzard ties the tank threat increases to Blessing of Salvation being removed Blizzard.
  • Slam cooldown 18 s → 15 s; Improved Slam now −2.5/5 s Blizzard.
  • Arms: Improved Tactical Mastery 3 ranks (keep 5/10/15 more Rage on stance swaps); Two-Handed Weapon Specialization +2/4/6% damage; Bloodthrill 2 ranks (10/20% chance per main-hand hit on a Rend target to allow Overpower) Blizzard.
  • Fury: Enrage to row 3; new Reckless Swings (row 4, 1 point, 15 s cooldown, costs 25% of base health: next 4 melee attacks give 5 more Rage, 8 s); Gore Drinker to row 5, now hanging from Blood Craze; Blood Craze and Gore Drinker heals no longer cause threat; Death Wish no longer needed for Flurry; Flurry 3 ranks (+10/20/30% attack speed for 3 swings after a crit); new Improved Whirlwind (row 6, 2 ranks: Whirlwind −1/2 s cooldown, +15/30% damage to the closest enemy); Bloodthirst now needs Death Wish; Booming Voice also cuts Piercing Howl's Rage cost Blizzard.
  • Victory Rush tooltip now shows its real damage Blizzard. Tauren Warriors now start with +5 Strength over Human Warriors (was +2) Blizzard.
  • Class damage is raised across the game and higher-level creatures get more health; no big tuning is planned before the raids open next month except bugs and outliers Blizzard.
  • Live since 3 Nov: the server hotfixes carry the Warrior talent changes, except Defiance and Defensive Stance, which still read 5/10/15% and 30% datamined (unconfirmed). The hotfix also removed Raging Blows (not in the notes) datamined (unconfirmed). This guide's builds use the live tree.

3. What changed for Rogues

Blizzard's 2 Nov development notes (posted 3 Nov)

  • Shank (new, level 24): 5 Energy, 10 s cooldown, melee off-hand strike for 50% weapon damage that cannot miss and generates 15 Energy; rank 2 at 44 generates 25 Blizzard.
  • Poised (new passive, level 18): out of combat at maximum Energy, your next Energy ability costs 10 less Blizzard.
  • Deadly Poison attack power coefficient 0.0045 → 0.009; Instant Poison 0.005 → 0.01 Blizzard.
  • Assassination: Murder removed; Lethality 2 ranks (+10/20% crit damage); Vigor to row 3; new Blood Splatter (row 3: Rupture +6 s, Garrote +9 s); Relentless Strikes to row 5 with 5 ranks (4–20% per Combo Point to restore 25 Energy); Venom to row 5, now a passive where Shank raises poison damage by 30% and poison chance by 20% for 6 s; Seal Fate 3 ranks (33/66/100%); Mutilate to row 7 at 90% weapon damage Blizzard.
  • Combat: Improved Eviscerate renamed Coup de Grace (Eviscerate and Death Mark +7/13/20%); Improved Sinister Strike renamed Efficient Strikes (Sinister Strike and Hemorrhage −3/5 Energy); Improved Sprint to row 2; new Composure (row 3, Poised +5/10); Flawless Execution −15 Energy; Weapon Expertise and Aggression to row 4, Aggression now +5/10/15% to Sinister Strike and Eviscerate; Dual Wield Specialization to row 5 with 2 ranks (+25/50% off-hand damage); new Flash of Steel (row 6, 5 ranks: Shank +3 to +15 Energy and −0.4 to −2 s cooldown) Blizzard.
  • Subtlety: Setup to row 1 (15/30/45%); Opportunity 5 ranks (+4–20% to Backstab, Garrote, Ambush and Mutilate); Dirty Deeds and Camouflage to row 2 (Camouflage 3 ranks); Premeditation and Serrated Blades to row 3; Elusiveness and Ghostly Strike to row 4 (Ghostly Strike lets your next Ambush or Garrote within 7 s skip Stealth); new Quick Getaway (row 4: one more Sap target, +15/30% speed for 3 s); Heightened Senses to row 5; Hemorrhage also boosts Garrote; new Boot Knife (row 6: Shank 20–100% chance to add 2 Combo Points); Death Mark is the row-7 finisher (25 Energy, 20 yd, 10 s cooldown, stores damage for 6 s, Garrote and Rupture at double rate); Quietus, Cutthroat, Thousand Cuts and Improved Distract removed Blizzard.
  • Bug fix: Hemorrhage and Ghostly Strike no longer deal much more damage than intended with a dagger Blizzard.
  • Live since 3 Nov: the server hotfixes carry the Rogue rework, and build 1.60.1.70755 (all regions) teaches Poised at 18 datamined (unconfirmed). Not in Blizzard's notes: top-rank poisons carry more charges (Instant Poison VI 250, was 175; Deadly 240, was 180; Wound 240, was 160; Mind-numbing 225, was 150) datamined (unconfirmed).

How to read this guide

Every claim carries a tag. Blizzard can still change anything before or after launch. The beta never went past level 30, so no level-60 Hunter has been tested. Every level-60 build, rotation and stat order here is theorycraft, built from talent tooltips.

  • Blizzard Confirmed in an official Blizzard article, blue post or beta development note.
  • Client data Read from the beta client or server data by a fan database; not announced by Blizzard.
  • Community Community build or consensus from fan guides; tested only up to the level-30 beta cap.
  • Theorycraft This guide's reasoning. Not tested at level 60, because the beta never went past level 30.
  • Unverified Assumed, carried over from Classic, or not confirmed for Forever. Check it in game.

Pets

  • Pets get extra stats from your gear, and can no longer receive player buffs that raise their stats. Blizzard
  • Pets use your melee or ranged crit chance, whichever is higher, and benefit from Expertise (fixed 15 Oct). Blizzard
  • Every family learns Bite or Claw (some both) and Dive or Dash, and has one unique ability. Foxes are a new family. Owls are now 'Bird of Prey'. Blizzard
  • You can't tame Beasts above your level. Aggressive mode is back (spellbook, pet tab). Blizzard
  • Known display bugs: Ferocity crit doesn't show on the character sheet, and pet hit/expertise scaling isn't shown yet. Blizzard
  • Blizzard (Forever Podcast ep. 2, via classicwowforever) plans more stable slots and more ways to unlock them. No date yet. Unverified

Strengths and weaknesses (Blizzard's own summary)

  • Longest range of any class. The pet can tank in solo and small-group play. Blizzard
  • 8-yard dead zone for ranged abilities. No stun (except talents and pets) and no interrupt. Traps only at your own location. Blizzard

  • Crit is close to even. With uses on the pull and every 180 s, Night Elf is ahead on fights under 150 s, Human between 150 and 195 s, and Night Elf again from 195 s (second use) until they're even at 300 s. On very long fights Elune's Light averages toward 10% × 15 ÷ 180 = 0.83%, just under Ruthless. Either way the gap is a fraction of a crit point. Theorycraft
  • Night Elf's case: Elune's Light lines up with Rapid Fire (+40% attack speed), trinkets and Bestial Wrath. Extra crit is worth more inside a haste window because more shots land in those 15 s, so stacked buffs beat their average. This is a raid-boss burst argument. Theorycraft
  • Human's case: Ruthless is always on, so it covers trash, dungeons, adds and the gaps between cooldowns. It doesn't depend on timing or the fight lasting long enough. +10% Spirit helps Forever's 'regen while casting' talents (Bestial Discipline, Rapid Recuperation, Resourcefulness), but Hunters carry little Spirit, so the absolute gain is small. Theorycraft
  • Whether pets inherit Ruthless (pets inherit your crit in Forever) is unverified. If they do, BM Humans gain a little more. Blizzard (pets inherit crit) / Unverified (Ruthless specifically)
  • Survival: Night Elf's +1% dodge slightly increases Mongoose Bite procs (it lights up after your dodge), but it's too small to decide the race. Expose Prey is the main enabler. Client data / Theorycraft
  • Dwarf note: Artisan's Precision +1% hit is a real PvE alternative. Until you reach the hit cap, 1% hit removes 1% of misses, worth about as much as 1% crit or more. The Forever hit cap isn't published. Theorycraft / Unverified

Battlegrounds: Human vs Night Elf Theorycraft

  • Human clearly wins. Will to Survive (3 min cooldown) breaks stuns: Warrior Charge/Intercept, Rogue Cheap Shot and Kidney Shot, Paladin Hammer of Justice. For a ranged class that must keep distance, one stun break often decides the fight. Blizzard / Theorycraft
  • Perception reveals stealthed Rogues and Druids near flags and nodes before they open. Blizzard
  • Night Elf: Elune's Light gives a 15 s burst kill window, Shadowmeld can drop targeting and threat, and Quickness adds run speed. All useful, but none of them outweighs a stun break. Theorycraft

Spec choice

Beast Mastery is the community's recommended spec for solo leveling. The pet tanks, Focused Fire and Unleashed Fury add damage, and from level 25 Summon Hawk adds two hawks. Marksmanship levels well if you want your own shots to do the damage and to drink less (Efficiency). Lone Wolf is better saved for groups. Survival works as a melee/trap spec from level 30 (Aspect of the Beast, Lacerate, Strider Kick). Before 30 its melee kit is thin. Community

Leveling rotation

  1. Before the pull: Aspect of the Hawk, Hunter's Mark on the target (Trueshot Aura too if MM). Blizzard's 2 Nov values put Hawk at +20 to +110 RAP and Trueshot Aura at +20 to +100. Blizzard
  2. Send the pet first and give it a moment to build threat. Turn Growl off in groups.
  3. Start Auto Shot and stand still for 0.5 s before each shot. Kiting shot-by-shot is slower than in Classic Era/SoD. Blizzard
  4. Serpent Sting only on enemies that will live most of its 15 s (MM: it also starts Rapid Recuperation).
  5. Arcane Shot when ready. BM: cast Summon Hawk until 2 hawks are up (it shares Arcane Shot's cooldown), then go back to Arcane Shot.
  6. Aimed Shot on single targets when you can stand still. Multi-Shot on 2+ targets. They share a cooldown.
  7. Mend Pet early, at about 50% pet health. Rapid Fire on elites.
  8. If something reaches you: Concussive Shot or Wing Clip, step out of the 8-yard dead zone, and drop Frost Trap if a pack chases you. Feign Death (level 30) to reset a pull. Remember to stand up.
  9. SV at 30+: Aspect of the Beast, Lacerate on long fights, Raptor Strike and Strider Kick on cooldown, Mongoose Bite after dodges, Wing Clip on runners.

Stats and gear while leveling

  • Ranged weapon damage is the biggest upgrade for BM/MM. Agility > Attack Power > Hit/Crit (combined stats in Forever) > Intellect. Intellect is worth more than in Classic because Mana runs out first, and MM's Careful Aim turns Int into AP. Community / Theorycraft
  • SV melee wants weapon DPS in both hands. Dual-wield from level 20, and Predator's Edge boosts off-hand damage. Client data / Theorycraft
  • Armor: leather until mail at level 40. Hunters can't use maces. Client data

H = (1 + quiver%) × (1 + 0.40 if Rapid Fire) × (1 + 0.30 if Deadly Aspects proc) × (1 + 0.02 if Skyborne Wind Blessed, 26 Oct pass) × …
Auto Shot interval  I = S ÷ H
Free-movement window per Auto cycle  M = I − 0.5 s   (move only during this window or the next Auto slips)
Auto Shots per 6 s cooldown window  = 6 ÷ I
Aimed Shot cycle (cast + cooldown)  = T_aim + 6 s = 8 s at T_aim = 2.0 s
Sniper Shot cycle  = 4 s + 15 s = 19 s   ·   Arcane Shot cycle (MM, IAS 2/5) = 5.4 s (+ GCD wait)
Raptor Strike interval (on-next-swing, 6 s cooldown)  = M_s × ceil(6 ÷ M_s),  M_s = melee speed ÷ H

3 · Clipping rules (what costs you Auto Shots)

  1. Moving inside the last 0.5 s before an Auto Shot delays it until 0.5 s after you stop. Loss = (time you stop + 0.5 s) − time the Auto was due. Move straight after an Auto Shot fires, never just before one. Blizzard (rule) / Theorycraft (advice)
  2. Aimed Shot and Sniper Shot don't block Auto Shot: it fires during the cast. So Classic's 'Aimed Shot clips Auto' problem shouldn't happen. Unverified: whether finishing the cast resets the Auto timer. To test, fire Aimed Shot right after an Auto Shot. If the next Auto still comes S ÷ H after the previous one, nothing resets. Blizzard / Unverified
  3. Instant shots (Arcane, Multi-Shot since 16 Oct, Serpent Sting, Summon Hawk, Concussive) only use the GCD. In Classic Era instants don't reset the Auto timer. Assume the same here until tested. Unverified
  4. Old Multi-Shot clipping is gone: Multi-Shot was a 0.5 s cast until build 70425 and is now instant. Client data
  5. Channels: Volley (6 s, no cooldown now) and Mend Pet (5 s). In Classic Era, channelling interrupts Auto Shot. Forever behaviour is unverified, so budget a Volley as 6 s without Autos until you test it. Unverified
  6. Disengage, Scatter Shot and Feign Death turn off auto-attack, so restart Auto Shot straight away (a macro or the new auto-attack/Auto Shot switching option helps). Client data / Blizzard
  7. Dead zone: you can't fire inside 8 yd. Every step out also triggers the 0.5 s stand-still before the next Auto. Blizzard
  8. Melee weaving as BM/MM doesn't pay off. You have to step into the dead zone (no Autos), Raptor Strike is an on-next-swing attack with a 6 s cooldown, and stepping back out costs another 0.5 s stand-still. Blizzard put Raptor Strike back to on-next-swing after an instant version made melee weaving too strong. Client data / Unverified / Theorycraft

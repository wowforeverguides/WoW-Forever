🏹 Orc Beast Mastery Hunter — 1-60 Route

WoW: Forever · balanced pace · Leatherworking + Skinning
Version v2026.11.03 · Last updated: 3 November 2026 (sources checked 28 & 31 October and 3 November 2026; Blizzard's 2 November development notes added)

One line per step: level, zone, hubs, notes. Tagged claims carry a badge: Verified Classic data Unconfirmed. Anything above level 30 should be re-checked after the 4 November 2026 launch.

Overview

Launch: 4 November 2026, 3pm PST (5 November, 00:00 CET). Level cap 60. The beta only went to level 30, so everything above 30 is Classic data or client data.
Start: Orcs start in the Valley of Trials (Durotar), not the Echo Isles. Echo Isles is a quest destination at ~8-10 Verified.

2. Hunter Notes & Talents

Pet quest & taming

Pet quest at L10 Verified (Thotar, Razor Hill 51.9, 43.5): "Taming the Beast" x3 with the Taming Rod - Dire Mottled Boar (~55.4, 40.1), then Surf Crawler (~60.1, 24.4; dismiss the boar first), then Armored Scorpid (~50.2, 36.6), hand in to Thotar each time; the third teaches Tame Beast. Then "Training the Beast" → Ormak Grimshot, Valley of Honor, Orgrimmar (66.1, 18.5), at ~12; reward Beast Training.

  • Tame Beast only works on beasts at or below your level Verified. Taming channel length and armor penalty are Classic rules Classic data: clear nearby mobs first.
  • Best early Durotar pets: Venomtail Scorpids, Bloodtalon Scythemaws near Rhinag Verified.
  • Pets scale with your gear (part of your attack power, your full crit); buffs from other players don't work on pets. 18 families incl. the new Fox: Vuldren (L6) and Vuldren Alpha (L10) on Zephras Isle Verified.
  • Orc racials Verified Oct 26-27 dev notes: Command is back (+5% pet damage), Blood Fury cooldown is now 2 minutes (was 3), Savage removed, weapon-specialization racials stay removed. Blood Fury: +10% attack power and spell power for 15 sec; Hardiness: stuns 20% shorter; new Shatter Curse Verified client 70707. Blizzard's Nov 2 development notes list these racial changes again as final Verified.

Hunter tuning (launch notes) Verified Blizzard Nov 2 development notes, posted Nov 3 03:20 UTC: the late-October Hunter nerfs are partly reversed. Aspect of the Hawk ranks 1-6 now give 20/35/50/70/90/110 ranged attack power; Trueshot Aura ranks 1-5 give 20/35/50/75/100. Aimed Shot bonus damage up about 110%; Arcane Shot base damage up about 30% (attack power coefficient 0.10 to 0.15); Serpent Sting base damage up about 20% (attack power per tick 3% to 5%). Lightning Reflexes 3-15% Agility, Barrage 5/10/15%, Sniper Shot bonus doubled. Pets keep their innate attack speed passive when you untrain them. Pathfinding is in row 3, which the talent order above already follows. Blizzard also raised the health of higher-level creatures to match the class damage increases, so above ~30 kills may not feel faster Verified. Launch-realm data from Nov 3 carries the same Hunter numbers per community trackers Unconfirmed until checked in game.

5. Professions: Leatherworking & Skinning

Skinning: free from the book Pelt Collecting for Beginners, a reward choice of Galgar's Cactus Apple Surprise (L3, Valley of Trials) Verified. Junior Skinning trainer Kagil, Valley of Trials (40.8, 67.9); Skinning Knife from Kagil or Duokna Verified. Orgrimmar trainers: Skinning Thuwd (63.4, 45.4), Leatherworking Karolek (62.8, 44.2) Verified WowCamp beta. foreverdb's Classic-data list names Kamari (Orgrimmar) and Waldor (Barrens) for Leatherworking Classic data.

Beasts of Thunder Ridge (L10, Kamari, Orgrimmar) rewards a Leatherworking pattern, no XP Verified.

Ranks: Apprentice 1-75, Journeyman 75-150 (train at skill 50), Expert 150-225 (skill 125), Artisan 225-300 (skill 200) Verified. Character level requirements (L5 / L10 / L20 / L35) are Classic trainer data Classic data.

Creatures can only be skinned once Verified. Camp Chair (Skinning 20), Camp Tent (LW 20). Field Guide (Skinning 140) and Tanning Rack (LW 140) Verified.

6. Balanced Pacing

  • XP curve matches Classic; no level scaling Verified.
  • Quest zones for main XP; one dungeon per ~5 levels: RFC (13), Ruins of Lordaeron (15), WC (17-20), SFK/BFD (24-28), City of Dalaran (30, after attunement), Drowned City (35), Krol'dok (40), Maraudon (46), Alcaz (48), Blackmaw Hold (55).
  • Skin everything, keep your pet fed. Slowest stretches: Barrens 17-24 and the 40-50 band.
  • XP boosts: Cozy Sleeping Bag (+3% for 2h), well-fed (+5% kill XP), Camp Tent (tops Rested up to 5% of a level) Verified.
  • As of Nov 3 ~17:15 Europe/Berlin: Blizzard's Nov 2 development notes (posted Nov 3 03:20 UTC) are the last pre-launch notes. For leveling: quest XP values and some quest level requirements and prerequisites were adjusted across the world, but not itemized Unconfirmed; respawns are faster in low-level zones, fastest in starting zones, and Blizzard will slow them again after the launch rush Verified. Bags no longer equip themselves: put each new bag on by hand Verified. Training every class ability from 1 to 60 now costs about 206-207 gold in total Verified. Client build 1.60.1.70753 appeared Nov 2 per community trackers Unconfirmed. Launch maintenance is scheduled Nov 4 18:00-23:00 UTC; realms open Nov 5 00:00 Europe/Berlin Unconfirmed community hotfix log.
  • Re-check at launch: everything above L30, Orc routing after Durotar, the Nov 2 Hunter numbers in game, which quests had their XP or level changed, Shen'dralas and Hyjal level ranges, Horde access to Excavation Site/Deadmines, class quest locations, starting arrow count.

1. Overview and shared rules

  • Same professions as Classic. Forever has the same twelve professions as Classic. Mining is a gathering profession and Smelting is part of it, not a separate profession; you smelt bars at a forge. Blacksmithing and Engineering are crafting professions. Confirmed
  • Rank gates. Each rank needs a minimum skill: Journeyman at 50, Expert at 125, Artisan at 200.
    • Beta testers report character-level gates for the crafting professions: Apprentice level 5, Journeyman 10, Expert 20, Artisan 35. These can't be checked above the beta's level-30 cap. Unconfirmed
    • Mining guides say the higher ranks also need character levels, but haven't confirmed the numbers. Classic-inferred
  • Grey sooner than Classic. Many Blacksmithing recipes stop giving skill-ups sooner than in Classic, typically about 25 points earlier, so don't follow Classic leveling guides. Datamined
  • Pairing. Mining feeds both Blacksmithing and Engineering. For a Hunter, the usual endgame pair is Engineering + Mining; for levelling it's Leatherworking + Skinning (see the companion Leatherworking & Skinning page).

No AH gold prices are given. The beta had no reliable price data, Forever launches with brand-new empty auction houses (one per ruleset pool), and Blizzard has not announced an auction API for Forever. Any number now would be a guess.

Merchant's Favor

  • Earning Favor: fill Waylaid Crates and hand them in.
    • 8 of the 30 crate types take ore or bars, for example Apprentice Ore (20 Copper or Tin Ore) or Apprentice Ingots (20 Copper, 20 Tin or 10 Bronze Bar).
    • Bar crates pay double the Favor of ore crates: 10 vs 5 at Apprentice, 20 vs 10 at Journeyman.
    • The Parts crate takes Engineering parts.
  • Vendors:
    • Horde: Gor'mak (Blacksmithing) and Fizzlefuse (Engineering) at Durotar Supply and Logistics, west of the Crossroads.
    • Alliance: Stondry Darkhammer and Fritz Fizzle at Three Corners, Redridge.
  • Engineering Favor schematics:
    • 45 Favor: SAF-T bombs and similar.
    • 180 Favor: Bent / Dented / Floppy / Stuckbutton Goggles and others.
    • 270 Favor: Gnomish Army Knife, Emergency Field Cloak, Loot-A-Rang.
    • 360 Favor: Gnomish Poultryizer, Ultralight Goblin Glider and others.
  • Blacksmithing: 61 plans for 30-120 Favor.
  • Titles: at skill 300, a certification for 1,000 Favor gives an account-wide profession title. The Engineering certification is listed in vendor data. Datamined
  • Craftsman's Writs (Blacksmithing, Engineering and others) ask for one crafted item and give reputation (75 / 125 / 200) plus silver. Examples:
    • Blacksmithing: Huge Thorium Battleaxe, Ebon Shiv.
    • Engineering: Crafted Solid Shot ×200, Sniper Scope.

Legacy

  • Legacy points: crafting professions (Blacksmithing, Engineering) give 1 account-wide point the first time a character reaches 150, 225 and 300. Mining gives none. Confirmed
  • Perks:
    • Working Overtime: +4% skill-up chance per rank, 5 ranks.
    • Bountiful Harvest: +20% Scarce materials per rank, including Pyrite and Bauxite from veins.
    • Performance Bonus: 5/10/15% chance of double crate Favor.
    • Dedicated Study: +1 to your lowest tradeskill, 23h cooldown.
    • Reagent Economy: Tier 1 camp objects cost no reagents.
    Datamined (values)
  • Bartering no longer discounts training or repairs (16 Oct). Confirmed

Related: Heart of the Mountain (Enchanting)

  • New formula items (30 Oct hotfix, now in client build 70753): Formula: Frozen, Molten and Dormant Heart of the Mountain. All three need Enchanting 265 and are rare and bind on pickup. The trinkets were already in the client; only the formula items were missing. Datamined
  • Mining link: each trinket uses 1 Blood of the Mountain, a gem from Dark Iron Deposits, plus 3 Small Brilliant Shard and one Alchemy essence: Undeath (Frozen), Earth (Molten) or Water (Dormant). They must be made at the Black Forge in Blackrock Depths. Datamined
  • Trinket stats: all three are item level 55, wear level 50, with +7 to four resistances. Molten adds +2 weapon damage and a use effect of +55 attack power for 15 sec, doubled in volcanic areas. Frozen adds 0.9% hit and a Frost/Shadow spell damage use, doubled in snowy areas. Datamined
  • New Enchant Boots - Speed: 8% movement speed, taught by Formula: Enchant Boots - Speed (Enchanting 280, uncommon, tradeable). Enchant Boots - Minor Speed drops from 8% to 4%. The enchant and its formula are in client build 70753 as new entries. Datamined
  • Enchanting page: this guide set has no Enchanting page yet.

New materials

  • Pyrite (copper, tin, silver, iron veins) becomes Sulfuric Acid (Alchemy).
  • Bauxite (gold, mithril) becomes Sandpaper (Engineering). New Blacksmithing plans from skill 200 to 270 use it.
  • Pitchblende (source unknown) becomes Azerothium Bar.
  • Heavy Thorium Ore (Mount Hyjal) becomes Heavy Thorium Bar.
  • Fel Crystal plus Heavy Thorium Bar make a Legionite Bar (Alchemy transmute).
  • One guide reports Alchemy transmutes (iron to gold, mithril to truesilver) on a 1-hour cooldown, which could flood the market with Gold and Truesilver Bars. Unconfirmed

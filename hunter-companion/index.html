Learn a pet ability, then teach it to your cat

All steps below come from community reports. Community

Learn a rank (with a temporary beast)

  1. Stable your cat first. Community reports say you can't tame while you have a pet, even a dismissed one.
  2. Use Beast Lore (from level 24) to see which abilities and ranks a wild beast knows. You can only tame beasts at or below your Hunter's level. That limit is separate from rank limits: reports say a beast can't keep a rank above its own level, and to teach a rank your cat must be at least that rank's pet level.
  3. Tame it and fight easy mobs. Turn off its other abilities so it uses the one you want over and over.
  4. Open Beast Training and check the rank is listed as learned before you get rid of the teacher. Use the game's own abandon/release option.

If you aren't learning it: make sure the beast really knows that rank (check with Beast Lore again), check you don't already know it, and give it more uses or longer fights.

Teach it to your cat

  1. Take your cat back from the stable and open Beast Training.
  2. To teach a rank, the cat's family must be able to use the ability, the cat must be at least that rank's pet level, and it needs enough training points. A pet trainer can untrain for a fee.

Pet trainer, for every family: Growl, Great Stamina, Natural Armor and the resistances. Learned from tames: Bite, Claw, Dash and Prowl. Community Cower: sources disagree. Unverified

Your cat: keep or replace

  • Default: keep your current cat. Learning new ranks is what keeps it current.
  • Cat kit: Bite and Claw start at rank 1; the pet levels for higher ranks are unverified. Dash and Prowl have 3 ranks each. Prowl starts at pet level 30; Dash at 30 or 32 (unresolved). Datamined (unconfirmed)
  • No verified benefit has been found for replacing your current cat just for attack speed. Unverified
  • When you untrain a pet, it keeps its built-in attack speed. Official notes
  • Planned 10 Nov pet changes have been reported, but we have not verified the primary announcement. Nothing here assumes them. Unverified

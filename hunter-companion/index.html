Learn a pet ability, then teach it to your cat

Steps come from community reports unless a step is labelled otherwise.

Learn a rank (with a temporary beast)

  1. Stable your cat first. You can't tame while you have a pet out, and the stable holds 2 pets besides your active one. Community Datamined (unconfirmed)
  2. You can only tame beasts at or below your Hunter's level. Official notes That limit is separate from rank limits: a beast can't keep a rank above its own level (beta fix, 1 Oct), and your cat must reach that rank's pet level before you can teach it. Community
  3. Beast Lore (level 24) is meant to show what a beast knows, but beta testers reported it no longer showed abilities. If it shows nothing, tame the beast and check its spellbook. Unverified
  4. Fight easy mobs with it, and turn off its other abilities so it uses the one you want over and over. The "you have learned" message is easy to miss. Community
  5. Before you get rid of the teacher, check in Beast Training that the rank is listed as learned. Then use the game's own abandon/release option. Some beta reports, not confirmed live, said pets lost abilities after a relog, dismiss or flight, so check the pet's spellbook after travelling or relogging. Community

    6. If you aren't learning it: check that the beast really has that rank in its own spellbook, and that you don't already know it. Then give it more uses or longer fights. Community

    Teach it to your cat

    1. Take your cat back from the stable and open Beast Training.
    2. To teach a rank, the cat's family must be able to use it, the cat must be at least that rank's pet level, and it needs enough training points. The point costs come from the server and are Unknown. A pet trainer can untrain for a fee. Community

    Pet trainer skills (any family): Growl, Great Stamina, Natural Armor and the five resistances. Community Great Stamina and Natural Armor rank 1: the client data gives pet level 1 or 10, so the level is unresolved; this page shows 10 for now. All pet rank levels here are datamined. Datamined (unconfirmed) Learned from beasts: Bite · Claw · Dash · Prowl. Cower: sources disagree on whether it comes from the pet trainer or from beasts; unresolved. Unverified

    Where the pet trainers are: current Horde locations in Forever are unresolved; ask a city guard or look in your capital. Unverified

    Your cat: keep or replace

    • Default: keep your current cat. Learning new ranks is what keeps it current.
    • Cat kit: Bite and Claw, 8 ranks each, at pet levels 1, 8, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48 and 56. Prowl and Dash, 3 ranks each, at pet levels 30, 40 and 50. Datamined (unconfirmed) One beta tester could not teach Dash until 32; unresolved. Unverified
    • Whether a cat can learn Bite above rank 2 is untested by the community. Unverified
    • No verified benefit has been found for replacing your current cat just for attack speed. Testers reported that pets of one family deal about the same damage whatever their speed, and Blizzard has not commented. Unverified
    • Replace the cat only for a reason you can check in game: a different family's ability you want, or a cat at or below your level that you prefer. Keep temporary teachers separate; release them once their rank shows as learned.
    • When you untrain a pet, it keeps its built-in attack speed. Official notes
    • Blizzard announced on 6 Nov that Hunter pets will get a hotfix on 10 Nov to tank about as well as in Classic Era. No numbers were given and it isn't live yet, so nothing here assumes it. Official notes

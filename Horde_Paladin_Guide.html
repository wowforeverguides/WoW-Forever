1. Overview, Ruleset & Paladin Races

Race / faction answer: In WoW Forever, Paladins are available to both factions. Horde: Undead (Forsaken) only. These are the first Horde Paladins; they train at Bandarion Keep in Tirisfal Glades. Alliance: Human and Dwarf. Tauren, Orc, Troll and Skyborne (either faction) cannot be Paladins, and there are no Blood Elves in Forever. Blizzard’s Deep Dive panel lists Undead Paladin among the six new race/class combinations; classicwowforever.com, WowCamp and WoW Forever DB all list the same three races. This guide is written for Horde (Undead) players, with Alliance notes where the routes differ.
Ruleset: World of Warcraft: Forever is Blizzard’s standalone “Classic+” branch on the modern client (beta patch 1.60.1, interface 16001). It is set after Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom and before Molten Core, uses reworked Classic-style talent trees and has a permanent level cap of 60. It is NOT a straight 1.12 re-release.

3. Leveling (1–60)

Spec choice

Recommended: Retribution. Every beta guide rates it the easiest and fastest solo spec: keep a Seal up, Judgement and Holy Strike on cooldown, heal between pulls with Holy Light instead of eating. It levelled so well at the beta caps that it was nerfed twice (WoW Forever DB). Protection is fine if you mainly level through dungeons (Skycoach), but slow solo before 30. Holy can level as a Holy Shock hybrid but is slower solo. Dual spec (Overview: at launch vs level 40) lets you quest as Ret and keep Prot or Holy for groups. WoW Forever DB rates Retribution B (3.50) ahead of Protection B (3.25) for leveling (30 Oct) community.

Leveling rotation

  • Blessing of Might on yourself; Seal of Righteousness early, Seal of Command from 20.
  • Judgement and Holy Strike on cooldown. Seal of the Crusader first on long fights, judge it, then swap back; with Sacred Arbiter, Holy Strike keeps the Judgement up.
  • Hammer of Justice then Judgement under Seal of Command: Judgement of Command does double damage to a stunned target.
  • Judge Seal of Justice on runners (stops NPC fleeing). You have no slow.
  • Use mana as a second health bar: top up with Holy Light and pull again. Save Lay on Hands for a bad pull.
  • Stand at the edge of melee range: in beta, enemies could parry and block attacks from behind at close range (known issue unverified / needs confirmation after launch at launch).

Ganking & defense

  • No Charge, Blink or slow: pick fights near cover, avoid open-ground duels with Hunters and Mages, never start below half mana.
  • Consecration reveals stealthed Rogues and Druids.
  • Divine Intervention (30, needs Symbol of Divinity, 1 h cooldown) can save a friend from a gank at the cost of your own life.
  • Free Action Potion and the Greater Insignia trinket matter more for a non-Human Paladin (no racial stun break). Insignia: removes loss of control, 5 min cooldown (beta tooltip).

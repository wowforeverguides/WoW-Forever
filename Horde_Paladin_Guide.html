1. Overview, Ruleset & Paladin Races

Race / faction answer: In WoW Forever, Paladins are available to both factions. Horde: Undead (Forsaken) only. These are the first Horde Paladins; they train at Bandarion Keep in Tirisfal Glades. Alliance: Human and Dwarf. Tauren, Orc, Troll and Skyborne (either faction) cannot be Paladins, and there are no Blood Elves in Forever. Blizzard’s Deep Dive panel lists Undead Paladin among the six new race/class combinations; a community site, a community site and a community database all list the same three races. This guide is written for Horde (Undead) players, with Alliance notes where the routes differ.
Ruleset: World of Warcraft: Forever is Blizzard’s standalone “Classic+” branch on the modern client (beta patch 1.60.1, interface 16001). It is set after Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom and before Molten Core, uses reworked Classic-style talent trees and has a permanent level cap of 60. It is NOT a straight 1.12 re-release.

2. Leveling (1–60)

Spec choice

Recommended: Retribution. Every beta guide rates it the easiest and fastest solo spec: keep a Seal up, Judgement and Holy Strike on cooldown, heal between pulls with Holy Light instead of eating. It levelled so well at the beta caps that it was nerfed twice (a community database). Protection is fine if you mainly level through dungeons (a community list), but slow solo before 30. Holy can level as a Holy Shock hybrid but is slower solo. Dual spec (Overview: at launch vs level 40) lets you quest as Ret and keep Prot or Holy for groups. a community database rates Retribution B (3.50) ahead of Protection B (3.25) for leveling (30 Oct) community.

Leveling rotation

  • Blessing of Might on yourself; Seal of Righteousness early, Seal of Command from 20. Righteous Fury (from 10) only when you tank: it adds 33% to all your threat Blizzard.
  • From 40 with Unsealed Wrath: let three Seal of Command copies build up (15 s), then switch to another Seal; your next hit releases all three, each with Seal of Command’s normal chance to fire Blizzardtheory.
  • Judgement and Holy Strike on cooldown. Seal of the Crusader first on long fights, judge it, then swap back; with Sacred Arbiter, Holy Strike keeps the Judgement up.
  • Hammer of Justice then Judgement under Seal of Command: Judgement of Command does double damage to a stunned target.
  • Judge Seal of Justice on runners (stops NPC fleeing). You have no slow.
  • Use mana as a second health bar: top up with Holy Light and pull again. Save Lay on Hands for a bad pull.
  • Stand at the edge of melee range: in beta, enemies could parry and block attacks from behind at close range (known issue unverified / needs confirmation after launch at launch).
  • Wands need 0.5 s standing still and get no spell damage (dev notes).

Quest & XP tips

  • XP curve matches Classic (Blizzard: 20.5 h to level 20 vs 20.4 h in Classic). Dungeon quests pay much more XP than Classic; the bonus was halved on 1 Oct, and dungeon creature XP was raised about 20% on 8 Oct.
  • XP extras: Cozy Sleeping Bag (+3% for 2 h), well-fed food adding 5% kill XP (Bear Brisket, Cooking 175), Legacy perks Well Rested and Talented.
  • Faster respawns were reported in Hillsbrad, Ashenvale, Wetlands, Duskwood and Thousand Needles.
  • Group finder is a listing tool only (no automatic Dungeon Finder); every listing needs a Playstyle tag.

Key PvP mechanics

  • Hammer of Justice is your only interrupt: 6 s stun at the rank learned at 54, 1 min cooldown (45 s with Improved Hammer of Justice). Shares stun DR with Cheap Shot, Charge, Intercept, War Stomp etc.
  • Repentance (Ret 21): 6 s incapacitate, Humanoids only, breaks on damage, 1 min cooldown, 20 yd. Different DR category from Hammer → up to 12 s of chained control. Works on Undead/other players (Humanoids) but not on Druid animal forms, Ghost Wolf or pets.
  • Every Paladin spell is Holy school: one Kick, Counterspell, Shield Bash or Earth Shock locks out heals, Hammer and even Divine Shield. Voice of Truth was removed on 2 Nov Blizzard: bait interrupts with a short Flash of Light and save Holy Light for when the kick is down theory. Divine Precision (Holy row 4) shortens Silences on you by 10/20% Blizzard.
  • Proc roots now have diminishing returns (Blizzard, 2 Nov): Improved Hamstring, Frostbite, Entrapment, Improved Wing Clip and Counterattack Blizzard. Server data puts them in one shared group without Frost Nova datamined (unconfirmed). Blessing of Freedom removes all of them client.
  • Forbearance: Divine Shield, Divine Protection, Blessing of Protection and Templar’s Bulwark all apply 1 min Forbearance — they are alternatives, not a chain. Sacred Duty 2/2 shortens Forbearance by 10 s Blizzard.
  • Seals, Blessings, Righteous Fury and Divine Favor are dispellable Magic (Purge, Dispel Magic, Devour Magic, Shield Slam). Templar’s Bulwark is dispellable Magic since 2 Nov Blizzard. Divine Shield cannot be dispelled.
  • Blessing of Freedom: 10 s (16 s with Guardian’s Favor), 20 s cooldown; slows and roots only, not stuns. Cast it in response, not pre-emptively vs Shamans.
  • Exorcism / Holy Wrath / Turn Undead do not work on Undead players (they count as Humanoids), but shred Warlock demons.
  • Holy Shock 20 yd enemy / 40 yd friendly; Hammer of Wrath 30 yd under 20% health.
  • PvP crits are no longer reduced (8 Oct dev notes).

Ganking & defense

  • No Charge, Blink or slow: pick fights near cover, avoid open-ground duels with Hunters and Mages, never start below half mana.
  • Consecration reveals stealthed Rogues and Druids.
  • Divine Intervention (30, needs Symbol of Divinity, 1 h cooldown) can save a friend from a gank at the cost of your own life.
  • Free Action Potion and the Greater Insignia trinket matter more for a non-Human Paladin (no racial stun break). Insignia: removes loss of control, 5 min cooldown (beta tooltip).

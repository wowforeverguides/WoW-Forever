The Gunnery Range: Rotations & Shot Timing

Level-60 PvE rotations per spec, shot timing, clipping and theory

⚠
Nobody has played a level-60 Forever Hunter yet (the beta stopped at level 30 and closed on 23 Oct 2026). The ability values here come from the client and Blizzard. The priorities, timelines and math are this guide's theorycraft. Every example uses made-up numbers that are labelled as examples. No level-60 logs exist. Theorycraft

Blizzard's rotation target. On WoW: Forever Podcast episode 2 (1 Oct 2026), Kris Zierhut: "you want to have three or four buttons, ideally. And more than that gets to be more things to keep track of than we want in the classic game" (abilities under 10 s count; longer cooldowns come on top). Aidan Moon, same episode: "you'll typically be pressing a single button as your core fallback ability", broken up by procs and short cooldowns, and "we do want time for things like consumables, seeing somebody in danger, seeing a mob running". Quotes checked against the episode's captions; Moon is named as the speaker of the first line in a speaker-labelled transcript. Blizzard By that definition Sniper Shot (15 s cooldown) is a cooldown, not a core button. Theorycraft

2 · Shot timing: formulas

H = (1 + quiver%) × (1 + 0.40 if Rapid Fire) × (1 + 0.30 if Deadly Aspects proc) × (1 + 0.02 if Skyborne Wind Blessed, 26 Oct pass) × …
Auto Shot interval  I = S ÷ H
Free-movement window per Auto cycle  M = I − 0.5 s   (move only during this window or the next Auto slips)
Auto Shots per 6 s cooldown window  = 6 ÷ I
Aimed Shot cycle (cast + cooldown)  = T_aim + 6 s = 8 s at T_aim = 2.0 s
Sniper Shot cycle  = 4 s + 15 s = 19 s   ·   Arcane Shot cycle (MM, IAS 2/5) = 5.4 s (+ GCD wait)
Raptor Strike interval (on-next-swing, 6 s cooldown)  = M_s × ceil(6 ÷ M_s),  M_s = melee speed ÷ H

Haste stacking is assumed multiplicative, as in Classic Era. Nobody has confirmed how quiver haste, Rapid Fire, Deadly Aspects and Wind Blessed (2% after the 26 Oct racial pass, not yet in the client files) stack in Forever. Forever quiver values also aren't verified. Whether haste shortens the 2 s Aimed Shot or 4 s Sniper Shot cast is unverified (in Classic Era ranged haste did shorten Aimed Shot), so plan with T_aim = 2.0 s and treat 2.0 ÷ H as the upside case. Cooldowns are fixed tooltip values and don't change with haste. Unverified

Theory: haste makes Auto Shots more frequent but shrinks the time you can move between them. During Rapid Fire plus a Deadly Aspects proc you get about 0.9 s of movement per shot, so spend cooldowns while standing still. A Monte Carlo run of this example (10% proc per Auto, 12 s buff that refreshes) puts Deadly Aspects uptime at about 40%. That's roughly +12% Auto Shots on average, which is why Deadly Aspects 5/5 opens every PvE build. Rapid Fire with Rapid Killing 2/2 (3 min cooldown) covers 15/180 = 8.3% of a fight, about +3.3% Auto Shots on average, and more if you use it on burn phases. Theorycraft

3 · Clipping rules (what costs you Auto Shots)

  1. Moving inside the last 0.5 s before an Auto Shot delays it until 0.5 s after you stop. Loss = (time you stop + 0.5 s) − time the Auto was due. Move straight after an Auto Shot fires, never just before one. Blizzard (rule) / Theorycraft (advice)
  2. Aimed Shot and Sniper Shot don't block Auto Shot: it fires during the cast. So Classic's 'Aimed Shot clips Auto' problem shouldn't happen. Unverified: whether finishing the cast resets the Auto timer. To test, fire Aimed Shot right after an Auto Shot. If the next Auto still comes S ÷ H after the previous one, nothing resets. Blizzard / Unverified
  3. Instant shots (Arcane, Multi-Shot since 16 Oct, Serpent Sting, Summon Hawk, Concussive) only use the GCD. In Classic Era instants don't reset the Auto timer. Assume the same here until tested. Unverified
  4. Old Multi-Shot clipping is gone: Multi-Shot was a 0.5 s cast until build 70425 and is now instant. Client data
  5. Channels: Volley (6 s, no cooldown now) and Mend Pet (5 s). In Classic Era, channelling interrupts Auto Shot. Forever behaviour is unverified, so budget a Volley as 6 s without Autos until you test it. Unverified
  6. Disengage, Scatter Shot and Feign Death turn off auto-attack, so restart Auto Shot straight away (a macro or the new auto-attack/Auto Shot switching option helps). Client data / Blizzard
  7. Dead zone: you can't fire inside 8 yd. Every step out also triggers the 0.5 s stand-still before the next Auto. Blizzard
  8. Melee weaving as BM/MM doesn't pay off. You have to step into the dead zone (no Autos), Raptor Strike is an on-next-swing attack with a 6 s cooldown, and stepping back out costs another 0.5 s stand-still. Blizzard put Raptor Strike back to on-next-swing after an instant version made melee weaving too strong. Client data / Unverified / Theorycraft

4 · Value per cast: why the priorities look the way they do

Example assumptions, not game data: bow speed S = 3.0 s, average bow damage per hit W = 140, quiver +15% ranged attack speed, RAP = 1200, no ammo bonus, no crits, no misses, target in range, you never move, 1.5 s GCD, cooldowns start when the cast finishes. Theorycraft Normalized weapon speed for Aimed and Sniper Shot is assumed to be N = 2.8 s (the TBC ranged value; Forever's normalization isn't published) Unverified. The Auto Shot formula W + RAP/14 × S is the Classic Era community formula and is unconfirmed for Forever Unverified.

  • Serpent Sting first (if the target lives 15+ s). It does the most damage per GCD and per Mana, its ticks can crit, and it isn't snapshotted (it recalculates on each tick). For MM it also starts Rapid Recuperation, so Mana regen continues while casting, and makes Rapid Killing proc when the target dies. Client data / Theorycraft
  • Aimed Shot before Arcane Shot before Sniper Shot. When two are ready, cast the one whose delay costs less. Delaying Sniper by 1.5 s (one Arcane GCD) loses 1.5/19 ≈ 8% of a Sniper Shot. Delaying Arcane by 4 s (one Sniper cast) loses about 4/6.9 ≈ 58% of an Arcane Shot. Delaying Aimed by 4 s loses 4/8 = 50% of an Aimed Shot. So Sniper Shot is the filler you slot into a gap, ideally one that ends just as Aimed Shot comes off cooldown. Theorycraft
  • Auto Shot keeps firing through the 2 s and 4 s casts, so long casts are nearly free as long as you stand still. The real cost is GCD and cast time that other shots could have used, plus how long you're stuck in place. Blizzard / Theorycraft
  • Slow ranged weapons do more per hit (W), and W goes into Aimed and Sniper Shot in full, while Auto Shot DPS stays the same at equal weapon DPS. A slow weapon also gives a longer free-movement window (M = I − 0.5 s). Prefer the slower of two bows with similar DPS. Theorycraft
  • Talent multipliers change the numbers but rarely the order: Lone Wolf +20% applies to everything. Barrage +15% (was +10% before 2 Nov) boosts Aimed/Multi/Volley. Improved Stings +20% boosts Serpent Sting. Mortal Shots adds +30% crit damage to all ranged abilities. Ranged Weapon Specialization adds +5% ranged weapon damage. Client data / Theorycraft
  • Multi-Shot vs Aimed Shot (shared cooldown): Multi-Shot's tooltip doesn't give a damage number. On 1 target, use Aimed Shot (its bonus is +350 since Blizzard's 2 Nov change, was +166). On 2–3 targets, use Multi-Shot (3 hits for an instant GCD). On 4+ stacked targets, add Explosive Trap and Volley. Theorycraft / Unverified

  6. Immolation Trap at your feet, which is under the boss for a melee Hunter. Use it every 24 s (30 s base, −20% from Survivalist's Discipline 1/2). The trap triggers when an enemy 'approaches', and whether it goes off on a boss that's already standing on it is unverified. Use Explosive Trap on trash packs. Resourcefulness 2/2 makes traps and melee abilities 60% cheaper. Blizzard / Client data / Unverified
  7. Rapid Fire also speeds up melee (+40%). Use it on pull or in a burn phase. Deterrence (5 min, −20% with Survivalist's Discipline) is a defensive cooldown, not a damage one. Client data

Weapon speed and Raptor Strike (theory): because Raptor Strike waits for the next swing after its 6 s cooldown, its real interval is M_s × ceil(6 ÷ M_s). A 3.0 s main hand fires it every 6.0 s (no waste). A 2.0 s weapon also gets 6.0 s. A 2.6 s weapon gets 7.8 s, losing about 23% of possible Raptor Strikes. Haste changes M_s, so the best weapon speed shifts during Rapid Fire. This assumes the cooldown starts when the strike lands, which isn't verified for Forever. Theorycraft / Unverified

GCD 1.5 s. Priority: Lacerate if missing → Mongoose Bite if usable → Strider Kick. Raptor Strike on the first main-hand swing after its cooldown. Theorycraft

8 · Leveling rotation (short)

  1. Aspect of the Hawk → Hunter's Mark → send the pet → Auto Shot. Community
  2. Serpent Sting only on mobs that will live long enough. Arcane Shot (BM from 25: Summon Hawk until 2 are up). Aimed Shot when you can stand still for 2 s. Multi-Shot on 2+. Community / Theorycraft
  3. If something reaches you: Concussive Shot or Wing Clip → step out of the 8 yd dead zone → wait 0.5 s for the stand-still, then Auto Shot. Frost Trap at your feet on packs (now usable in combat). Blizzard / Theorycraft
  4. Rapid Fire on elites. Mend Pet at about 50%. Feign Death (level 30) to reset a pull. Community
  5. SV at 30+: Aspect of the Beast, Lacerate on long fights, Raptor Strike and Strider Kick on cooldown, Mongoose Bite whenever it lights up. Community

9 · Battleground rotation (ranged / utility)

For the MM/SV control build (0/31/20) or BM (31/0/20) in instanced battlegrounds. Damage is the same priority as PvE, but movement and control take priority. Theorycraft

  1. Open at max range: Hunter's Mark → Sniper Shot from up to 45 yd (4 s cast; the next 3 Shots get +10 yd for 10 s) → Serpent Sting → Arcane Shot → Aimed Shot only while nobody is on you. Blizzard / Client data / Theorycraft
  2. On healers: Viper Sting (drains 1,108 Mana over 8 s, 15 s cooldown; Improved Stings cuts up to 6 s). Scorpid Sting (−2% hit) on melee attackers. Client data
  3. When someone closes in: Concussive Shot (12 s cooldown, 50% slow 4 s) → Scatter Shot (30 s cooldown, 4 s disorient, turns off your auto-attack) → Freezing Trap at your feet or Frost Trap (all traps share one 30 s cooldown since 2 Nov) → step back → restart Auto Shot (0.5 s stand-still). Entrapment roots anyone who triggers a trap. Blizzard / Client data
  4. Fire trap under yourself on flag carriers or node defenders only when you will not need a Freezing Trap in the next 30 s (shared trap cooldown since 2 Nov). Flare (15 s cooldown) on stealthed enemies near flags and nodes. Blizzard / Client data
  5. Defensives: Deterrence (+25% dodge/parry for 10 s, 5 min), Feign Death (30 s) to drop targeting, Disengage (5 s, turns off auto-attack). Client data
  6. Rapid Fire / Bestial Wrath on a kill target your team is already focusing. Intimidation (BM) as a 3 s stun to peel or set up a kill. Client data / Theorycraft
